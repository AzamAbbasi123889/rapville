QUICK ANSWERS BEFORE WE TALK

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still unsure whether RAPVILLE fits your project? Start here, or ask the site assistant in the bottom corner.

What does RAPVILLE actually do?+

RAPVILLE's offer centres on artist content strategy, filming and editing. The goal is to build a joined-up short-form content system around your music, rather than a batch of disconnected posts.

Can you guarantee a viral video?+

No one can honestly guarantee virality. The work is to improve the creative inputs—positioning, hooks, concepts, execution and iteration—and learn from audience response. Case studies are examples of past outcomes, not a promise of yours.

Which artists and genres do you work with?+

The inquiry is open to artists, artist managers, creative teams and labels. Share your genre and goals in the form so the team can assess whether the approach fits your project.

Which platforms do you make content for?+

The content plan can consider Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. The best mix depends on your audience, content style and campaign objective.

Where can you shoot?+

RAPVILLE's current materials list production crews in Los Angeles, New York, Miami, London, Dubai and Europe. Share where you are based and what you need so availability can be confirmed for your project.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing depends on the scope of strategy, production and editing needed. The form asks for an optional working budget to help start a realistic conversation; the site does not publish a fixed price list.

What should I bring to a strategy call?+

Your artist or project link, a track or upcoming release date, examples of content you like, and one or two goals are enough to start. You do not need a polished campaign brief.

How do I get started?+

Complete the growth inquiry above. The team can review your details and contact you about next steps. Once a scheduling link is configured, the confirmation screen can also send you directly to book a time.
