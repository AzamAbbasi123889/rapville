WEBSITE NOTICE

Privacy notice

Setup note: This is a launch-ready draft, not legal advice. RAPVILLE should review and replace the contact/controller details and retention terms below with its confirmed business information before publishing the site publicly.

This draft describes how information may be handled by the RAPVILLE GitHub Pages demo. RAPVILLE should confirm the controller details, lawful basis, retention period and final service providers before using the site for real leads.

What information may be collected?

When you submit an enquiry, the site may collect your name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, artist or project name, role, goals, genre, preferred platform, release timeline, budget range, public social or music link, message, source page, and the date the enquiry was received. The chatbot asks for your name, email and phone number before it answers questions in the chat.

Why is it used?

Information is used to respond to your enquiry, assess whether RAPVILLE's services may fit the project, arrange a follow-up, prevent spam, and operate the website. If an AI chat provider is configured, your question and limited recent chat context may be sent to that provider to generate an answer. Contact details are designed to be excluded from the AI chat request.

Where is information stored?

In the unconfigured GitHub Pages demo, test submissions are stored in the visitor's browser local storage when the browser permits it; they are not sent to RAPVILLE. When a form endpoint is added to config.js, submitted fields are sent to that third-party service, which may process and store them under its own terms. A configured chat endpoint receives the visitor's questions and recent chat context, but the chatbot is designed not to include the contact details in the AI message payload. The site owner must verify the actual endpoints and privacy practices before launch.

Retention and sharing

Browser-local demo entries can remain on that device until cleared by the visitor or browser. A configured external form service or backend may have separate retention rules. RAPVILLE should set a clear retention period and explain any sharing with form, hosting, email, CRM or AI providers before accepting real submissions.

Your choices

You can use the on-page FAQ without submitting the enquiry form or sharing details with the chatbot. To request access, correction or deletion of information already submitted, contact RAPVILLE using its verified business contact channel. Add the correct privacy contact email before launch.

Cookies and analytics

The demo does not add advertising or analytics cookies by default. If analytics, advertising pixels, embedded scheduling tools, or other tracking services are added, update this notice and implement any consent mechanism required for the relevant visitors.
